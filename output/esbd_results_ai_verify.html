This report uses the live paginated ESBD backend service to collect the full current open-solicitation result set, scores that full listing set, and then looks up a generous shortlist through the ESBD details service to extract required fields such as title, solicitation ID, contact information, posting date, due date, classification, and attachment links.

Relevance is determined by weighted keyword and fuzzy matching against the vendor service categories from the take-home brief. Matches in solicitation titles and classification codes count more heavily than weaker description-only signals, while clearly unrelated terms such as software-only or legal-service signals are penalized.

This implementation favors a straightforward HTTP-first scraper because the required ESBD data is available through the public site and its live JSON services without browser automation. For a smaller high-confidence subset of candidates, the scraper also downloads PDF attachments, classifies them as text-based or likely scanned based on extractable text, and folds extracted PDF text back into ranking. If a Gemini API key is supplied, the final results can also include AI-generated summaries based on that PDF text.
