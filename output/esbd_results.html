Methodology

This report uses the live paginated ESBD backend service to collect the full current open-solicitation result set, then fetches each solicitation detail page to extract required fields such as title, solicitation ID, contact information, posting date, due date, classification, and attachment links.

Relevance is determined by weighted keyword and fuzzy matching against the vendor service categories from the take-home brief. Matches in solicitation titles and classification codes count more heavily than weaker description-only signals, while clearly unrelated terms such as software-only or legal-service signals are penalized.

This implementation favors a straightforward HTTP-first scraper because the required ESBD data is available through the public site and its live JSON listing service without browser automation. Some additional relevance signals may still live inside attached PDFs, which are linked here but not yet parsed in this MVP.
